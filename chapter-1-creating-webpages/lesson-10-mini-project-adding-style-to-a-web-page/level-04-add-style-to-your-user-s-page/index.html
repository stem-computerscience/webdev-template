Willow Grove Animal Shelter

At Willow Grove Animal Shelter, we are dedicated to rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming animals in need. Our mission is to provide a safe haven for homeless pets and to work tirelessly towards a world where every animal is loved and cared for. Through community engagement, education, and compassion, we strive to create lasting bonds between people and pets.

Volunteer Information

Introduction to Volunteering

Join our dedicated team of volunteers at Willow Grove Animal Shelter and make a difference in the lives of animals. Whether you have a few hours a week or can commit more time, your help is invaluable.

Roles and Responsibilities

  1. Animal Care
    • Feeding
    • Grooming
    • Exercising pets

  2. Event Assistance
    • Helping with fundraising events and adoption drives

  3. Shelter Maintenance
    • Assisting with cleaning and upkeep of the shelter

Time Commitment

Flexible schedules available, ranging from a few hours per week to full-time volunteer work.

Educational Resources

Pet Nutrition Guide

  • Balanced Diet:
    • Dogs: High-quality dog food with a balance of protein, carbohydrates, fats, vitamins, and minerals.
    • Cats: Nutrient-rich cat food, high in protein with moderate fat levels, and low carbohydrates.

  • Feeding Schedule:
    • Puppies/Kittens: Feed 3-4 times a day.
    • Adult Dogs/Cats: Feed 1-2 times a day.

  • Special Diets:
    • Consult a veterinarian for pets with specific dietary needs such as allergies, obesity, or health conditions.

  • Hydration:
    • Ensure fresh water is available at all times.

Training Information

  • Basic Commands:
    • Teach basic commands like sit, stay, come, and heel through positive reinforcement.

  • Socialization:
    • Expose pets to different people, animals, and environments to develop well-adjusted behavior.

  • Behavioral Training:
    • Address issues like excessive barking, chewing, or scratching through consistent training and professional advice if needed.

  • Training Resources:
    • Recommend professional trainers or training classes for more in-depth guidance

Grooming Information

  • Coat Care:
    • Dogs: Regular brushing, depending on the breed. Bathing as needed.
    • Cats: Regular brushing to reduce hairballs, especially for long-haired breeds.

  • Nail Trimming:
    • Regular nail trims to prevent discomfort and health issues.

  • Ear and Teeth Cleaning:
    • Clean ears to prevent infections and brush teeth to promote dental health.

  • Professional Grooming:
    • Some pets may require professional grooming, especially those with longer coats
