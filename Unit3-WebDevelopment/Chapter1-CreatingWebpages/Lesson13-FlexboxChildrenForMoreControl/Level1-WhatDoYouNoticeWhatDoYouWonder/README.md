## Instructions

Can you spot some new stuff going on!

### Do This
- Resize the Preview Window to see items grow and shrink!

**In the HTML file:**
- Take a look at the items in the div container.
- Discuss with your neighbor — What do you notice? What do you wonder?

**In the CSS file:**
- Take a look at the rule sets below the comments that say "CHECK ME OUT!"
- Discuss with your neighbor — What do you notice? What do you wonder?
- Find the `width: 10%` property and change it to `30%`.
- Since `flex-shrink` will only affect items when there is not enough space in the container, this will allow you to see the 5th item shrink to allow room for the other items that are not supposed to shrink.

**Files:** `index.html`, `style.css`
