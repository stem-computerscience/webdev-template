Endangered Animals

Pangolin

Sometimes called "scaly anteaters", these nocturnal animals can curl up into a ball, similar to an armadillo. They are hunted for their scales which are used in traditional medicine in some parts of the world. They are actually the most trafficked mammal in the world.

Dhole

These animals are related to dogs and wolves, living in packs. Dholes are most commonly found in India and Southeast Asia. They are endangered due to many factors including habitat loss and loss of prey.
