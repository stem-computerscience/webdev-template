# Make a Recipe

Use your new image skills to share the recipe for a recipe that your family cooks for a holiday or other special occasion. Include images of the necessary ingredients.

# Do This
1) Think of a recipe and google search the ingredients
1) Upload pictures of the ingredients to Web Lab
1) Add the images to the webpage
1) Add the ordered list of steps to the webpage
