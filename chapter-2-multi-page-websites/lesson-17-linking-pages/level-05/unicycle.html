Unicycle

A one wheeled vehicle powered by a person, unicycles have gained a lot of popularity over the past couple of years. Typically known to be in circuses, the unicycle has now become used in a variety of different ways. With normally just a wheel, a frame, a seat, and pedals, unicycles are known to be more difficult to ride

Here are some more fun facts about unicycles

  • Many different types of sports have been created using unicycles (unicycle basketball. unicycle hockey, etc.)
  • There are different kinds of unicycles now too (mountain unicycles, indoor unicycles, touring unicycles, etc.)
  • Rupert Grint, who plays Ron Weasley in the Harry Potter movies, is an avid fan of unicycling
