Bicycle

A familiar vehicle to people, a bicycle, also known as bike or cycle, is a two wheeled vehicle that is human powered. They were first introduced in Europe in the 19th century (1800-1899) and by now there are more bikes used than cars in the world. For many parts of the world, bicycle are the main form of transportation and for many people, they are used for fitness and recreation. The creation of the bicycle forever changed society.
