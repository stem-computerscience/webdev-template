My Favorite Game

Ever since we were kids, my sister and I have been die-hard fans of the Super Mario games, a passion that Nintendo might not have originally targeted towards girls like us. But they certainly captured our hearts! Watching the 2023 Super Mario animated movie, with its cleverly inserted classic sound effects, was like a delightful journey down memory lane, filling me with an indescribable sense of joy and nostalgia.
