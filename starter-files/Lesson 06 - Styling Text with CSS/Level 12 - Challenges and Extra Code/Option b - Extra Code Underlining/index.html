How to make boba

Do you enjoy delicious boba tea?

Did you know you can make boba at home?

Making boba is easy. I always get the quick style that you can boil in just a few minutes.

Important!!!!!

These instructions are for the quick style boba. If you get the regular boba, they WILL NOT WORK!

Step 1

Boil your water and pour the boba in. Stir until they float to the top, then let them boil for 2-3 minutes

Step 2

Turn off the heat and let it sit in the water for another 2-3 minutes, then drain it and rinse it with cold water

Step 3

Pour brown sugar syrup or just brown sugar on top of your boba and let them sit for a long time so they can get very sweet.
