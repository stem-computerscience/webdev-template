Indoor Activities

Board games are a great indoor activity! Here are my top 3 favorites:

  1. Settlers of Catan

    2. One of my favorites is Settlers of Catan. It's a relatively simple game that can accommodate up to 6 players

  2. Blukus

    3. Blokus is a game of spatial reasoning and problem-solving, so everyone's mind gets a workout!

  3. Azul

    4. This is a fun, creative game where players take turns drafting colored tiles from suppliers to their player board. Later, players score points based on how they've placed their tiles to decorate the palace
