Tricycle

They're For Adults Too!

When many people think of tricycles, or trikes, they think of smaller children riding them so they don't fall.

And while that may be true, that is not their only use. In Asia, tricycles are quite common as they can have larger baskets on the back two wheels to carry things.

It is a favorable vehicle for children and older adults because of their stability. It can naturally stand upright rather than the rider having to keep the balance.
