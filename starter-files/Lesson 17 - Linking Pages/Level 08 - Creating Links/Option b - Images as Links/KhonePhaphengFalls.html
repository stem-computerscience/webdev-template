Khone Phapheng Falls

Picture by Hiroo Yamagata - Wikimedia Commons - CC-BY-SA

This is the widest waterfall in the world. It is in Cambodia, and it's part of the Mekong River. It may not look that high, but it's the main reason that boats can't sail up the river to get to China. Some people also believe that it's the largest waterfall by volume, but it's not that easy to measure waterfalls, so we're not sure yet.
